Project option: pathly
Problem: Students and early-career professionals often repeat the same information across profiles, CVs, portfolios, and career planning tools, while receiving unclear next steps.
What I built: Pathly is a bilingual, local-first web app that turns one saved professional profile into a CV, responsive portfolio, and transparent rule-based career guidance. It includes English/Arabic RTL support, light/dark themes, fictional sample data, browser-only storage, print-to-PDF CVs, and downloadable portfolio HTML.
Result: A polished, responsive, self-contained Pathly experience that works by opening index.html directly and keeps user-entered information in the browser.
